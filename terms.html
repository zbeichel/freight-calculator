5. What Counts as a Quote

A quote is one pickup–delivery pair that we look up automatic miles (the driving distance) for. Every different pair counts separately. For example:

  • Lancaster, PA → New York, NY is 1 quote.
  • Keeping Lancaster, PA and changing the delivery to Charlotte, NC is a 2nd quote.
  • Changing the pickup instead (say Harrisburg, PA → Charlotte, NC) is another quote.
  • Going back to Lancaster, PA → New York, NY later the same day does not count again.
  • Changing the weight, trailer, rate, margin or dates on a lane does not count.
  • On a multi-stop load, each leg (one stop to the next) counts as one quote.
  • Entering the miles yourself never counts.

Quote counts reset every day at midnight UTC (7 p.m. Eastern Standard Time, 8 p.m. Eastern Daylight Time). Free accounts include automatic miles on 5 quotes per day. Quotes through the API are counted separately, by billing month — see API Access.
